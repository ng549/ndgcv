Case study

FitBox — vacant box → shared operators

At Big Box Group: mid-size vacant retail is too big for independents and too small for consolidators. FitBox shrinks each lease to revenue rooms only, centralizes commons, and stacks operators who share a customer.

Problem

Empty boxes + independents crushed by capex, marketing, and labor. Prototype: Ashford Dunwoody ~38k sf bay.

Model

Airport logic: one desk, one locker core, managed retail, marketing fund, profit-share credits.
