# Collapsed band artwork
Built-in image generation, single asset, 2026-09-21. WebP is a delivery-format conversion of the generated PNG; no composition changes.

Prompt: One wide horizontal background artwork for collapsed navigation bands on an executive personal CV website. Flat editorial sketch aesthetic, subtle layered charcoal, deep midnight navy and desaturated blue slate pigment, fine hand drawn architectural and workshop lines suggesting connected ideas and practical making, abstract and understated rather than a literal scene. Dark throughout, soft tonal variation, no cream or white areas, no bright focal objects. Low visual complexity so small ivory headings remain legible. Edge-to-edge landscape artwork, no text, lettering, logos, UI or borders. Reused under alternating dark overlays.

Asset: docs/illustrations/collapsed-dark-art-v1.webp
Expanded sections retain their pre-existing artwork.
