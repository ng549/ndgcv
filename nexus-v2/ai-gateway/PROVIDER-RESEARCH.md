## Search and research routing

Treat search as a separately metered tool/capability. A route must declare whether citations are returned, whether browsing is provider-hosted or Nexus-controlled, search-call price, content retention, allowed domains, and whether retrieved text is isolated as untrusted input. Deep-research jobs require a job budget, source/citation validation and maximum tool-call count.

## Qualification sequence

1. Official product and terms evidence captured.
2. Exact plan/model/use case rights record created.
3. Privacy/retention profile passes route requirements.
4. Adapter contract fixture passes.
5. Representative Nexus workload benchmark passes quality, latency and successful-task-cost thresholds.
6. Outage, rate-limit, usage and reconciliation tests pass.
7. Control Tower approves entry into the relevant bullpen.
