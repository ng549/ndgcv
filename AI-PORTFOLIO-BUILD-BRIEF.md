# AI Portfolio Build Brief — ndgcv

## Objective

Redesign the existing AI section of Nicolas Goureau's CV website into a visually strong, evidence-backed **AI-Native Product Builder** portfolio section.

The site should make a potential employer, investor, operating partner, or technology partner understand within 20–30 seconds that Nicolas is not simply an executive who uses AI tools. He has developed a repeatable multi-AI operating model and uses it to research, architect, prototype, build, test, coordinate, and improve software products.

Primary positioning:

> **I am an operator who learned to turn AI into a development organization.**

Supporting positioning:

> **20+ years of operating experience, translated into software through multi-agent AI development.**

Do not present Nicolas as a conventional career software engineer. The differentiated story is: experienced operator + product thinker + systems architect + AI-native builder + multi-agent orchestrator.

# Visual Direction

The current CV site already has a strong editorial/operator character. Extend it rather than turning the site into a generic neon AI landing page.

Desired qualities:

- executive
- sophisticated
- editorial
- modern
- technically credible
- restrained but visually interesting
- strong information hierarchy
- dark/light contrast consistent with existing site
- rich project screenshots
- subtle motion only where it adds meaning

Avoid:

- glowing robot heads
- generic circuit-board imagery
- fake code rain
- excessive gradients
- meaningless AI particles
- stock-tech illustrations
- overuse of logos
- claims without evidence

The screenshots themselves should provide much of the visual energy.

# Copy Guardrails

Strong language is encouraged, but keep the evidence hierarchy accurate.

Good:

- AI-native product builder
- multi-agent AI orchestrator
- product architect
- systems designer
- AI-directed engineering workflow
- AI-assisted software development
- functional prototype
- real-time browser software
- enterprise platform architecture

Avoid unsupported claims such as:

- senior software engineer
- full-stack engineer with 20 years of coding experience
- production SaaS founder when the product is still a prototype
- autonomous AI company if the agents are not truly autonomous

The story should be impressive because the operating model and body of work are real, not because titles are inflated beyond recognition.

# Implementation Rules

1. Inspect the existing repo before changing code.
2. Preserve the current Experience story engine.
3. Build the new AI section responsively.
4. Reuse existing typography/design language where possible.
5. Keep JavaScript lightweight.
6. Final site assets used in HTML/CSS must live in GitHub, not depend on private Google Drive links.
7. Google Drive remains the master/original asset library.
8. If a source image needs cropping/compression, preserve the original in Drive and commit only the optimized derivative to GitHub.
9. Use descriptive filenames.
10. Do not hotlink private Drive assets from the public website.
11. Do not introduce secrets, API keys, or private URLs into frontend source.

# Acceptance Criteria

The redesign is successful when a new visitor can answer all of these within 30 seconds:

1. What kind of operator is Nicolas?
2. What does `AI-native` mean in his case?
3. How does he use multiple AI systems together?
4. What is PARKOUR?
5. What real products has he designed or built?
6. Which work is functional versus conceptual?
7. Which AI and development tools are part of his workflow?
8. Why does this make him more valuable to an employer or investor?

The final section should feel like **evidence of a new operating capability**, not a list of software logos.
