{{ secAboutBtn }}The person

Before the operator, the person.

Raised across three cultures with three passports — American, French, and Israeli — and the languages to go with them. Add decades of travel across Europe, Asia, and South America, and the result is someone genuinely open to new ideas and comfortable in any room: a factory floor in Asia, a boardroom in New York, a market stall anywhere. That range built an unusual skill — finding things no one else can find, whether it's a supplier, a product, a deal, or the one insight buried in the data. The other defining trait is relentlessness: when something needs to be figured out, it gets figured out, no matter how many iterations it takes. It's the same pattern that runs through the career — build the thing yourself, from the first store lease to a fashion company founded and run for a decade on nights and weekends alongside full-time executive roles, and now a portfolio of AI-native products built hands-on with the same stubborn curiosity. Off the clock, the energy goes to family, the kitchen, and anything with an engine.
